GENDRAGOONSSD — PRIVACY POLICY (PRE-RELEASE)
Last revised: October 14, 2026

Operator contact: grimvirusoffical@gmail.com. This preliminary policy describes the current development application. Before accepting paying customers, the operator must publish and legally review the final Terms and Privacy Policy.

1. SIGN-IN DATA. When you choose Google Sign-In, Firebase Authentication processes the authentication. GenDragoon receives a verified Firebase user identifier, email and short-lived identity token. The desktop sign-in session is kept in app memory, not embedded in the app package. Your Google password is never entered into GenDragoon.

2. TERMS RECORDS. We store your Firebase user ID as the path to your own Terms-acceptance record. Your accepted Terms version, your two affirmative acknowledgments, and the server-generated acceptance time are stored in Cloud Firestore. Firestore security rules prevent other ordinary users from reading or editing these records.

3. WORKSPACES. Project names, plan selections and local workspace drafts are saved on your device, separated by your verified Google account ID. Future paid workspaces may record quota, subscription and transfer usage on a secure backend.

4. STORAGE CONTENT. Existing personal-cloud connections remain separate from Firebase login. Customer cloud files will require independently scoped authorization when managed storage is launched. Uploaded content may include personal files chosen by the user. We will document retention and access procedures before activating managed storage.

5. PAYMENTS. When paid subscriptions become available, Stripe will process payment information. GenDragoon will handle billing identifiers and subscription status; it does not need your full card number.

6. PROCESSORS AND DISCLOSURE. Firebase/Google Cloud and Stripe may process necessary data under their respective terms. We may disclose information when legally required or necessary to protect customers and investigate abuse, using documented access controls.

7. SECURITY AND LIMITATIONS. We use authentication, role-based access, transport encryption and provider-managed storage encryption where supported. No system guarantees perfect anonymity or zero risk. Local apps may be inspectable on your own device.

8. ACCOUNT CONTROLS. You can sign out without accepting Terms. Requests for access, correction, deletion or privacy questions can be sent to the contact address above. Certain records may be retained as necessary for accounting, fraud prevention or lawful obligations.

9. UPDATES. Material changes to the policies may require renewed consent. An updated version and change date will be displayed within the app.

END OF PRIVACY POLICY.
