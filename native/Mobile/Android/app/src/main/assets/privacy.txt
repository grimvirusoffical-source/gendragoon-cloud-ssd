GenDragoon Android data-use notice — 0.22.0 development candidate
Updated October 26, 2026

Red-XAI sign-in opens the official browser approval page. Passwords are entered on Red-XAI only. The app keeps short-lived access credentials in memory and protects its refresh credential with Android Keystore encryption. Disconnect revokes the session where available; it preserves private vault keys, local transfer records and server content.

MachineNotes are stored on the GenDragoon server. The server receives account identity, MachineNote metadata, item names and other metadata needed to operate the service, encrypted visual-carrier PNG objects, billing entitlements, and request/security records. Uploads and restores are initiated by the local owner. Original selected files are retained. Android does not use Google/Firebase authentication or Google Cloud storage. The distribution platform and operating system may process their own diagnostic data independently.

Private storage secrets and encrypted private keymaps stay on this device unless you explicitly export an encrypted portable vault. Export contains encryption material, not account refresh/access credentials. Account sign-in cannot recover lost client encryption keys. An exported vault uses its own passphrase and is bound to the same Red-XAI account. Protect both the vault and passphrase.

Developer Teams exchange account-scoped usernames, membership, invitations, presence, signaling and chat metadata/text. Team membership grants no file, decryption-key or storage authority. Relayed chat is retained for seven days. Direct WebRTC peers may receive the network-address information required for a direct connection. This interface exposes no peer file-transfer or upload commands.

Storage subscriptions are managed outside this Android consumption-only interface. Android does not collect payment-card details or launch Stripe checkout. Existing active paid entitlements are checked by the service.

This candidate is not a completed Play Store submission. Store data-safety declarations, account-deletion access, signed release/device checks and a final published policy remain release gates. This notice describes the implemented client; it does not replace the account service's applicable terms and privacy policy.
