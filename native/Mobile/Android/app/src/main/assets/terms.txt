GenDragoon Android development candidate — service information
Version 0.22.0 (36), October 26, 2026

Red-XAI presents its applicable account terms in the browser approval flow. This Android candidate does not substitute a locally fabricated terms-acceptance record. Review the published service terms before using the account.

Only upload material you are authorized to store. Preserve independent backups. A transfer is complete only after full encrypted readback and verified success. Originals are retained on Android. Performance and platform compatibility vary, and no fixed transfer rate is promised.

An active MachineNote entitlement is required for new uploads. This consumption-only Android client has no purchase or MachineNote-creation interface. Subscription and cancellation terms are those disclosed by the service at purchase.

Private encryption keys are separate from account sign-in. Preserve your private vault and encrypted portable export; signing in again cannot recreate lost client keys. Team peers have no file authority. Android restores folder/macOS-application backups as verified archives; it cannot run macOS applications.

This is an unsigned development/release candidate until the documented build, signing, device, policy and store gates pass. See the data-use notice for implemented data handling.
